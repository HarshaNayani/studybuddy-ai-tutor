# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is how plants, algae and some bacteria turn light energy into chemical energy stored as glucose.

## Overall equation
6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy -> C6H12O6 + 6O2. Carbon dioxide and water go in; glucose and oxygen come out.

## Where it happens
It takes place in chloroplasts, which contain the green pigment chlorophyll. Chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green.

## Two stages
Light-dependent reactions happen in the thylakoid membranes. Light splits water, releasing oxygen and producing ATP and NADPH.
The Calvin cycle (light-independent) happens in the stroma. It uses ATP and NADPH to fix CO2 into glucose.

## Factors affecting the rate
Light intensity, CO2 concentration and temperature all change the rate. Too high a temperature denatures the enzymes and the rate drops.
